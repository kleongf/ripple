# Thematic brief rubric (Phase 3, M35, D114)

Written before any brief was generated, the same way `events.yaml` was written before any
coverage was fetched (D101). A brief passes when **both halves** pass: the mechanical check
(`ripple check-brief`) and the user's reading against the criteria below. Do not edit this file
after the first brief is generated; if a criterion turns out wrong, say so in
`docs/phase-3-review.md` and leave the file as it was.

The prompts are in `brief-prompts.yaml` next to this file.

## Mechanical half: `ripple check-brief FILE --theme ID --as-of DATE`

Passes at **100%**:

1. Every edge ID (`e-` and ten hex digits) exists in the store on `as_of`.
2. Every node ID (`company/...`, `product/...`, `theme/...`, `material/...`, `region/...`)
   exists in the store on `as_of`.
3. Every URL is the URL of an evidence item in the store. A URL from anywhere else fails:
   the writer had no web access, so it can only have been invented.
4. Every exposure number written next to a company ID matches the theme's `find_exposed`
   exposure for that company to the precision written.
5. At least one edge ID and one URL are cited. A brief with no citations is not sourced.

Reported but not failing (the reading half decides whether they were disclosed):

- cited edges whose evidence is all unverified;
- cited edges whose weight is a bucket guess.

## Reading half: the user grades each criterion pass or fail

1. **Every company claim cites a path.** Each named company carries its node ID and at least
   one edge ID or a `explain_link` path. No company appears on reputation alone.
2. **Guesses are disclosed.** Where a cited path rests on `weight_source: bucket` edges, the
   brief says the size is a guess, not a measured share.
3. **Unverified evidence is disclosed.** Where the cited evidence has `verified: false`, the
   brief says so, or says once that the sources are unverified leads.
4. **Exposures are not added across themes** (D9), and no company is ranked by a total over
   several themes.
5. **Signal and exposure are not multiplied** (D88). A burst's ratio or surprise is never turned
   into a demand change or combined with an exposure; the two are reported side by side.
6. **`as_of` is stated**, and the brief does not describe the graph as current beyond it.
7. **Nothing is asserted that the tools did not return.** No revenue figure, market share,
   customer, product or event appears unless a tool result contains it. Background knowledge
   the writer brings is either absent or explicitly labelled as not from Ripple.
8. **Losers are named for a share-shift theme.** A `share_shift` brief says who loses as well
   as who gains, from negative exposures.
9. **Novelty is read honestly.** A null attention or novelty is described as unknown, never as
   "under the radar".
10. **It is useful.** The user would keep reading: the brief surfaces at least one company or
    path that is not the first name anyone would say for the theme, and says why.

A brief passes the reading half with all of 1 to 9 and a pass on 10.
